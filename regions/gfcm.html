Mediterranean and Black SeaDraft

GFCM area of application, FAO Area 37

The Mediterranean and Black Sea combine an old, mostly small-scale fleet with a long record of overexploited demersal and small pelagic stocks. Overexploitation has fallen to its lowest level in a decade, and the General Fisheries Commission for the Mediterranean (GFCM) now gives quantitative advice for its key stocks through a harmonized summary sheet. This page describes the region's fisheries and stocks, the route from assessment to scientific advice, the tools in use with toolbox options, and case studies.

From assessment to scientific advice

GFCM members report data through the Data Collection Reference Framework. Stock assessments are prepared and reviewed in the working groups on stock assessment, validated by the Scientific Advisory Committee (SAC), and turned into recommendations by the Commission.

National data
Catch, effort, length and survey data reported through the Data Collection Reference Framework
→
Working groups
Stock assessments of demersal and small pelagic stocks, and the Working Group on the Black Sea
→
Subregional committees and SAC
Review and validation; scientific advice in a harmonized summary sheet
→
Commission
Recommendations: multiannual management plans, fisheries restricted areas, catch and effort limits

Since 2013 GFCM members have adopted 11 management plans and established 11 fisheries restricted areas. About 7.3% of the fleet (over 6 000 vessels) is authorized under GFCM plans and measures.1, 2 Stock status from GFCM assessments also informs FAO reporting through SoSI and SDG 14.4.1.

Advice framework with FLRef Placeholder

At the request of the SAC (2021 to 2023), a new summary sheet harmonizes how quantitative advice is reported for key stocks, particularly those under management plans. The SAC endorsed it, and it was tested in the working groups on stock assessment in 2024. Functions in the FLR package FLRef produce the sheet's tables and figures from the main assessment platforms.5

→

F-based short-, medium- and long-term forecasts, aligned with the management plan
→
Summary sheet
Advice table, status plots, forecasts, comparison with the previous assessment
  • Advice table. Headline advice with current levels, reference points, quantitative status and the F reduction needed: % F reduction = (1 − 1/Fratio) × 100.5
  • Forecasts. Short-term (intermediate year plus one), medium-term (about five years or to the end of the management plan) and, where rebuilding takes longer, long-term (about two generations).5
  • Comparison and partial F. A plot comparing the update with the previous assessment, and partial fishing mortality by fleet or gear for integrated models such as Stock Synthesis.5
  • Output to the STAR template. CSV files of time series and reference points for the GFCM stock assessment reporting template.5
  • User guides. Worked examples for a4a (European hake GSA 3, red mullet GSA 16), SPiCT (deep-water rose shrimp GSAs 17–18) and Stock Synthesis (European hake GSAs 17–18).5
  • Model summary sheets. Examples include European hake in GSAs 12–16 (Stock Synthesis) and round sardinella in GSAs 26 and 27 (SPiCT).6

Tools used in the Mediterranean and Black Sea

Assessment platforms used for GFCM quantitative advice, as listed in the FLRef user guides, with other methods in use.5 Select a data situation or a tool to see its limits and the toolbox option.

Data situation Tool or method What it does Caveats and limitations Tool recommendation
Catch-at-age a4a (FLa4a) Statistical catch-at-age model in FLR Needs catch-at-age and survey or CPUE indices. Results depend on the submodels chosen for F, catchability and recruitment. Keep. Report through FLRef; check with the model acceptance checklist.
Catch-at-age XSA Extended survivors analysis (virtual population analysis) Assumes catch-at-age is known without error. Sensitive to tuning indices and to the shrinkage settings. Prefer a4a or SAM where possible; report through FLRef.
Catch-at-age FLSAM (SAM) State-space assessment model Needs catch-at-age and indices. Process and observation variances can be confounded. Keep. Report through FLRef.
Integrated Stock Synthesis Integrated length- and age-structured model, multi-fleet Complex: many settings to agree; diagnostics are essential. Few national scientists run it. Keep for key stocks. Review with ss3diags2 and cross-check with JABBA3 and FLicc.
Catch and index SPiCT Surplus production model in continuous time Needs an index that tracks abundance. Priors on the shape and process error affect status. Keep, following the good practice guidelines. Cross-check with JABBA3.
Catch and index JABBA Bayesian surplus production model Needs an informative index and priors for data-poor stocks. Switch to JABBA3. Prepare indices with cpueTargeting.
Catch-only CMSY Catch-only stock status Prior-driven; not suitable for validated advice. Use CMSY+ in JABBA3 for screening only.
Length-based LBSPR, length indicators Spawning potential ratio and length-based indicators Assume equilibrium and representative samples; sensitive to M/K and asymptotic length. Use FLicc, including its length-based indicators.
Surveys MEDITS, MEDIAS and Black Sea surveys Trawl and acoustic surveys for demersal and small pelagic stocks Coverage and timing differ between areas and years. Use survey indices in JABBA3 and SPiCT; trends with JARA.

Data situation

The Mediterranean and Black Sea have more assessed stocks than most regions, but data gaps remain for bycatch, small-scale fisheries and the southern and eastern shores.

Data sources

Where stock and catch information comes from.

  • Data Collection Reference Framework. GFCM members report catch, effort, fleet and biological data through it.
  • Scientific surveys. Bottom trawl surveys for demersal stocks and acoustic surveys for small pelagics support the indices used in assessments.
  • Small-scale fisheries data. 19 GFCM contracting and cooperating parties had small-scale fisheries data systems in 2025, up from 8 in 2022.1
  • Stock assessment forms and summary sheets. Each validated assessment is documented in a standard form and, since 2024, the harmonized summary sheet.5

Gaps identified in SoMFi 2025

Challenges that the report lists for fisheries.

  • Discards. Bottom trawl discards are often 20 to 40% of the catch.1
  • Vulnerable species. Bycatch hot spots for elasmobranchs and sea turtles in the central and eastern Mediterranean and the Adriatic; cetacean bycatch mainly in Black Sea small-scale fisheries; stronger observer coverage needed.1
  • Compliance and fleet. Low compliance with measures, an ageing workforce, unequal profitability and energy costs up 32% for industrial fleets.1, 2
  • Depleted stocks. Sardine stocks have long been overexploited and still show biomass depletion; European hake recovery varies between subregions.2
