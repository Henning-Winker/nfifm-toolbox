From data to management advice

Stock assessment and management are iterative processes, in which data and assessments evolve continuously. The toolbox builds users' capacity to understand their data, run assessment methods themselves and with greater confidence, and turn results into management advice and action.

Within the three key steps, five workflow steps are required and the remaining steps are optional and can be repeated or skipped. Users can enter the route at any step. Each step links to its page.

One standard of good practice

Risk equivalence holds only if every assessment informing advice passes the same scrutiny. The toolbox adopts four objective properties of a plausible model (Carvalho et al., 2021), and no single diagnostic is used alone to accept or reject a model. Each property is checked at the workflow step where it matters most.

PlausibilityConsistency with biology and exploitation history: the overarching check

Fit to the data

Prediction skill

For surplus production models these properties are made operational in good practice guidelines (Kokkalis et al., 2024). The model acceptance checklist applies them across the toolbox.

Catalogue structure

Every method has a standardised catalogue entry that guides responsible use by documenting data needs, pre-processing, model assumptions, sensitivities, misuse prevention, and available resources. Each entry follows the three key steps of the workflow, from understanding data through running the method to management advice and action, with use and misuse guidance and case studies alongside.
