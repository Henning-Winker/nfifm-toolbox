Crosswalk

Cross-cutting attributes of all tools in the catalogue

The crosswalk lists every tool in the catalogue with its attributes. Use the filter boxes to screen tools; click a name to open its catalogue entry. It is not a ranking: its purpose is to make assumptions and data requirements explicit.

NoteSource of truth

Generated from data/methods.csv, which also drives the method selector and the attribute row on each method page. Edit one row and all three update on the next build.

The crosswalk lists every tool in the catalogue with its attributes. Use the filter boxes to screen tools; click a name to open its catalogue entry. It is **not** a ranking: its purpose is to make assumptions and data requirements explicit.

```{r}
#| echo: false
source("R/toolbox.R")
crosswalk_table(site_root = "../")
```

::: {.callout-note}
## Source of truth
Generated from [`data/methods.csv`](https://github.com/Henning-Winker/nfifm-toolbox/blob/main/data/methods.csv), which also drives the [method selector](../select/index.qmd) and the attribute row on each method page. Edit one row and all three update on the next build.
:::
