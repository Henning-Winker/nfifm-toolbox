Method overview

Field Entry
Family / class Biomass dynamic models → unstructured biomass dynamics (catch-only; BSM with an index)
Method name CMSY++ / CMSY+ (catch-only) and BSM (Bayesian state-space Schaefer model with an index)
Short description Runs a Schaefer production model for many r–k pairs drawn from prior ranges and keeps the pairs whose biomass trajectories neither collapse nor exceed carrying capacity and end within the assumed final depletion range; BSM adds an abundance index.
Main outputs MSY with uncertainty, r, k, B/BMSY and F/FMSY trajectories.
Typical use case Stocks with a long catch history and no reliable index; triage of many stocks; BSM where an index exists.
NoteOrigin of the guidance on this page

The pre-run checks, prior rules and expert questions follow the guidance for uses and misuses of data-limited methods developed by N. Gutierrez and K. Kleisner (2018) for the FAO online modules, updated here for CMSY++ and BSM.

Data requirements

Field Entry
Minimum data A catch time series of at least 20 years covering development, peak and decline, with the peak not in the first or last 5 years (after Gutierrez and Kleisner, 2018).
Preferred data An abundance index (for BSM); independent information on depletion at the start and end of the series (length data, CPUE, expert knowledge).
Optional auxiliary data Resilience category or life-history information for the r prior (\(r \approx 2M\), \(r \approx 2F_{MSY}\)).
Data quality considerations The catch series must be complete: missing periods of exploitation or under-reporting bias productivity and depletion directly.

Model documentation

Field Entry
Key parameters \(r\), \(k\), initial and final relative biomass (\(B/B_0\)), process error SD.
Priors / fixed inputs r from resilience: High 0.6–1.5, Medium 0.2–0.8, Low 0.05–0.5, Very low 0.015–0.1 (FishBase categories); k between max catch and a multiple of it; start and end depletion ranges from expert judgement or catch rules.
Error structure Process uncertainty via priors; lognormal i.i.d. process error in the stochastic version; observation error on the index in BSM.
State-space formulation BSM: yes. Catch-only CMSY: no (filtering of r–k pairs).
Implementation notes To be completed

Setting the priors: questions to experts

Prior Question to experts Rule of thumb
Start year From what year onward are the catch data reliable? The retained series must still be ≥ 20 years
Start biomass (\(B/B_0\)) Was the stock lightly fished, fully exploited or overfished at the start? Low 0.1–0.4, Medium 0.2–0.6, High 0.5–0.9
Intermediate biomass Is there a year when biomass was particularly low or high (change in exploitation, exceptional year class)? Optional intermediate prior
End biomass (\(B/B_0\)) Was the stock lightly fished, fully exploited or overfished at the end? Catch-ratio rule: last/max catch < 0.3 → Low (0.01–0.4); > 0.7 → High (0.5–0.9); otherwise Medium (0.2–0.6)
Resilience (\(r\)) What is the best range for adult natural mortality \(M\), or for \(F_{MSY}\)? \(r \approx 2M\); \(r \approx 2F_{MSY}\)

If start and end biomass are both judged low, a catch-only method should not be used.

Assumptions and sensitivities

Field Entry
Core assumptions Schaefer production; stationary productivity (constant parameters); complete catch series; depletion priors reflect the true start and end state.
Main sensitivities Lower bound of the r prior; start and final depletion priors; upper bound on k in developing fisheries.
Likely violations Incomplete or under-reported catches; catches driven by markets or regulation rather than abundance; developing fisheries without a peak; lightly exploited stocks.
Expected bias Evaluations found k overestimated by about 10% and r (and FMSY) underestimated, closer to F0.1 (after Gutierrez and Kleisner, 2018); with short or incomplete catch series, status can be biased in either direction.
Warning signs Posterior close to the depletion prior; r estimate at the lower prior bound; very wide range of viable r–k pairs.

Misuse prevention

WarningMisuse warnings shown by the method selector
  • Do not use: when catch = incomplete: Catch-only methods assume the catch series is complete. Under-reported or missing catches bias productivity and depletion directly.
  • Caution: when goal = catch advice: Catch-only status is driven largely by priors on depletion and resilience. It should not be the sole basis for catch advice.
  • Caution: when lifehistory = short-lived: Short-lived, recruitment-driven stocks fluctuate strongly; catch-only depletion priors are rarely informative for them.
  • Caution: when goal = HCR testing: An assessment fit alone does not test a harvest control rule. Use the assessment as the estimation model in an MSE (see FLR mse).
  • Do not use: when catch_pattern = short: Catch-only methods need at least about 20 years of catch covering development, peak and decline; shorter or flat series contain too little information on productivity (Gutierrez and Kleisner, 2018).
  • Do not use: when catch_pattern = increasing: In a developing fishery with still-increasing catches the peak has not been reached, so carrying capacity cannot be bounded from the catch.
  • Do not use: when catch_pattern = light: Lightly exploited stocks: the catch series carries almost no information on productivity.
  • Caution: when catch = reliable: Depletion priors derived only from the ratio of recent to maximum catch are assumptions, not data; set them with expert input or length and CPUE evidence where possible.

Rules are maintained in data/misuse_rules.csv.

WarningMisuse warnings shown by the method selector
  • Do not use: when catch = incomplete: Surplus production models treat catch as (near) complete removals. Reconstruct missing catches or add catch uncertainty before fitting.
  • Caution: when index_length = short: A short index relative to the catch history weakly identifies depletion and productivity. Use informative priors and test initial-depletion sensitivity.
  • Caution: when index_type = commercial: Commercial CPUE may be hyperstable or affected by changing targeting. Standardise it (see cpueTargeting) before use.
  • Caution: when species = mixed: Single-species production models ignore technical interactions; reference points may not be jointly achievable in mixed fisheries.
  • Caution: when goal = HCR testing: An assessment fit alone does not test a harvest control rule. Use the assessment as the estimation model in an MSE (see FLR mse).
  • Caution: when catch_pattern = increasing: With catches still increasing and little contrast, productivity and depletion are weakly identified; rely on the index and informative priors, and test initial depletion.

Rules are maintained in data/misuse_rules.csv.

Field Entry
Do not use when The catch series is shorter than about 20 years, flat, still increasing, or misses important periods of exploitation; the stock is lightly exploited; start and end biomass are both judged low.
Common misuses Using default depletion priors from the catch ratio as if they were data; reporting depletion from catch-only runs as an estimate (no data update the depletion prior); using catch-only status as the sole basis for catch advice.
Pre-use checklist To be completed
Minimum checks to pass before results are used for advice.
Pre-run checks Catch series length ≥ 20 years; peak not in the first or last 5 years; no continuous increase or decrease; expert questions on start, intermediate and end status answered; r range justified from \(M\) or \(F_{MSY}\) (after Gutierrez and Kleisner, 2018). Use the catch-series check below.
Advice suitability Triage and indicative status; catch advice only with an index (BSM) or supporting evidence.

Catch-series check

Paste the catch series (one year, catch pair per line) and answer the two expert questions. The check applies the pre-run rules for catch-only methods and suggests the depletion-prior category.

Example data are illustrative. Annual data are expected; the example uses every third year.

Applications and support

Field Entry
Applications / case studies Widely used for global and regional status screening, e.g. for SOFIA and Mediterranean stocks.
References Froese, R. et al. (2017) Estimating fisheries reference points from catch and resilience. Fish and Fisheries 18: 506–526; Froese, R. et al. (2023) The CMSY++ method. Acta Ichthyologica et Piscatoria 53: 173–189; Gutierrez, N.L. and Kleisner, K. (2018) Guidance for uses and misuses of data-limited methods (FAO working document).
Software / tools CMSY++ (R); Stock Monitoring Tool module (temporarily unavailable).

Suitable example

Catch series with a clear development, peak and decline over more than 20 years, with independent evidence of current depletion.

Assumption sensitivity

Alternative lower bounds of the r prior and alternative final-depletion ranges; compare with BSM when an index exists.

Unsuitable example

A developing fishery with still-increasing catches, or a series that starts at the peak: k and depletion cannot be bounded from the catch.

### Catch-series check

Paste the catch series (one `year, catch` pair per line) and answer the two expert questions. The check applies the pre-run rules for catch-only methods and suggests the depletion-prior category.

```{=html}

<label for="cc-data" style="font-weight:600">Catch series (year, catch)</label>
<textarea id="cc-data" rows="8" style="width:100%;font-family:monospace;font-size:.85rem">1985, 120

<div style="display:flex;flex-wrap:wrap;gap:16px;margin:.6em 0">
<label>Fishing pressure at the start <select id="cc-start"><option value="low">Low</option><option value="medium" selected>Medium</option><option value="high">High</option></select></label>
<label>Fishing pressure at the end <select id="cc-end"><option value="low">Low</option><option value="medium" selected>Medium</option><option value="high">High</option></select></label>
</div>
<div id="cc-out" class="sel-meta" aria-live="polite"></div>
<p class="sel-meta" style="margin-top:.6em">Example data are illustrative. Annual data are expected; the example uses every third year.</p>
</div>
<script>
(function () {
  function run() {
    var rows = document.getElementById("cc-data").value.split(/\n/).map(function (l) { return l.split(/[,;\t ]+/).filter(Boolean).map(Number); })
      .filter(function (r) { return r.length >= 2 && isFinite(r[0]) && isFinite(r[1]); }).sort(function (a, b) { return a[0] - b[0]; });
    var out = [], stop = false;
    function add(kind, msg) { if (kind === "stop") stop = true; out.push("<li><b>" + (kind === "stop" ? "Do not use:" : (kind === "ok" ? "Pass:" : "Caution:")) + "</b> " + msg + "</li>"); }
    if (rows.length < 3) { document.getElementById("cc-out").innerHTML = "Enter at least three years."; return; }
    var y0 = rows[0][0], y1 = rows[rows.length - 1][0], span = y1 - y0 + 1;
    var c = rows.map(function (r) { return r[1]; }), max = Math.max.apply(null, c), iMax = c.indexOf(max), yMax = rows[iMax][0];
    var ratio = c[c.length - 1] / max;
    if (span >= 20) add("ok", "series spans " + span + " years (" + y0 + "–" + y1 + ").");
    else add("stop", "series spans only " + span + " years; at least 20 are needed to cover development, peak and decline.");
    if (yMax - y0 < 5) add("stop", "the catch peak (" + yMax + ") is in the first 5 years: the development phase is missing, so depletion at the start is unknown.");
    else if (y1 - yMax < 5) add("stop", "the catch peak (" + yMax + ") is in the last 5 years: the fishery may still be developing, so carrying capacity cannot be bounded.");
    else add("ok", "catch peak in " + yMax + ", away from both ends of the series.");
    var up = 0, down = 0; for (var i = 1; i < c.length; i++) { if (c[i] > c[i - 1]) up++; else if (c[i] < c[i - 1]) down++; }
    if (up === c.length - 1 || down === c.length - 1) add("stop", "catches rise or fall throughout; no peak can be identified.");
    var endCat = ratio < 0.3 ? "Low (0.01–0.4)" : (ratio > 0.7 ? "High (0.5–0.9)" : "Medium (0.2–0.6)");
    add("caution", "last catch / maximum catch = " + ratio.toFixed(2) + ": catch-ratio rule gives final biomass prior <b>" + endCat + "</b>. This is an assumption, not an estimate; confirm it with expert, length or CPUE evidence.");
    var s = document.getElementById("cc-start").value, e = document.getElementById("cc-end").value;
    if (s === "low" && e === "low") add("stop", "start and end biomass both judged low: the catch series cannot separate productivity from depletion.");
    document.getElementById("cc-out").innerHTML = '<p style="margin:0 0 .3em"><b>' + (stop ? "Do not run a catch-only assessment with these data." : "Pre-run checks passed; continue with careful prior setting.") + "</b></p><ul class='sel-warn'>" + out.join("") + "</ul>";
  }
  ["cc-data", "cc-start", "cc-end"].forEach(function (id) { document.getElementById(id).addEventListener("input", run); document.getElementById(id).addEventListener("change", run); });
  run();
})();
</script>
```
