Overview

Field Entry
Family / class Multispecies and mixed fisheries → multispecies MSY and harvest strategies
Method name mmsyFM, multispecies MSY fleet model (R + Shiny): Henning-Winker/mmsyFM · documentation
Short description Simulates mixed fisheries in which fleet segments allocate fishing days across métiers by expected profit, on Schaefer stocks with seasonal availability. Finds the multispecies MSY with a safeguard for every stock and tunes and tests effort- and closure-based harvest strategies under uncertainty.
Main outputs Equilibrium effort by segment and métier; yield curves (shared q, profit-blind and profit-seeking fleets, closures); safeguarded MMSY for yield, revenue, profit or one stock; harvest-strategy comparisons with projections, trade-offs and colour-coded decision tables.
Typical use case Multispecies trawl and multigear fisheries where single-stock MSY targets cannot all be met at once; participatory workshops on effort caps and closures.

Shiny app for workshops

mmsyFM::run_app() opens an app for participatory workshops, with these tabs:

  1. Set-up: Atlantic example, Gulf of Thailand draft or uploaded tables; capacity, profit preference, daily costs.
  2. Equilibrium: days by segment and open métiers.
  3. Yield curves: shared q, profit-blind and profit-seeking fleets, closures.
  4. Safeguarded MMSY: total yield, revenue, profit or the catch of one stock, with every stock at or above Bpa.
  5. Harvest strategy: compare rules (no control, fixed MMSY effort, feedback rules, stock and métier closures) under uncertainty.
  6. Tune triggers.
  7. Management options and Guide.

Hosting on the iMarine gateway next to the Stock Monitoring Tool is under consideration.

1 · Data

Field Entry
Minimum data Four tables (read_ms_system()): species (species, code, group, r, K, price), metiers (metier, tactic, ground), catchability (metier × stock), segments (share, loyalty, efficiency, cost multiplier, vessel CV, home métier).
Preferred data Stock depletions from assessments for conditioning (ms_condition()); real prices and daily costs; habitat maps to derive catchability (ms_landscape()).
Optional auxiliary data Seasonal availability (season_amp, season_peak), persistence floor F_cap, starting depletion dep_init, segment capacity.
Data quality considerations To be completed
Highlights representativeness, consistency and screening needs.
Pre-processing steps Build or upload the four tables; tune the fleet to the open-access status quo (ms_tune()); condition stock status (ms_condition()).
Standardization needs To be completed
Identifies whether indices or samples require standardization.
Aggregation / binning choices To be completed
Documents how catches, ages or lengths should be grouped.
Fleet / gear handling Métiers are tactic × ground; segments have home métiers, loyalty, efficiency and costs; fishers choose métiers by expected profit, \(P(\text{métier}) \propto \max(\text{efficiency} \times \text{revenue} - \text{cost}, 0)^\gamma\).

sys <- atlantic_system()                                        # 10 stocks, 6 métiers, 3 segments
sq  <- ms_status_quo(sys, capacity = 1000, gamma = 1.5)          # open-access status quo
opt <- ms_optimise(sys, "yield", levers = "total", gamma = 1.5)  # MMSY with every stock >= Bpa
plot_kobe(list("Status quo" = sq, "Safeguarded MMSY" = opt), sys)

2 · Method

Field Entry
Software / tools remotes::install_github("Henning-Winker/mmsyFM"); Shiny app mmsyFM::run_app(). Authors: H. Winker, R. Amoroso, M. Pons, R. Sharma. Licence MIT.
Implementation notes Key functions: ms_status_quo(), ms_optimise(), hcr_effort(), ms_tune_hcr(), ms_perf(), plot_kobe(), plot_quilt(). Examples: atlantic_system() (10 stocks, 6 métiers, 3 segments) and got_system_draft() (Gulf of Thailand, 20 stock groups).
Key parameters Stock \(r\), \(K\), price; catchability matrix; segment loyalty, efficiency and costs; profit preference \(\gamma\); fleet capacity.
Priors / fixed inputs To be completed
Documents fixed values, priors and externally supplied assumptions.
Error structure JABBA-type process error scaled by \(r\) and depletion; assessment and implementation error, effort noise and data lags in closed-loop rules.
State-space formulation Simulation model (operating model), not fitted to data.
Core assumptions Schaefer production for each stock; catchability by métier constant over time; effort allocated by expected profit; stock parameters (\(r\), \(K\), depletion) taken from assessments.

3 · Outputs, diagnostics and sensitivities

Field Entry
Main sensitivities Catchability matrix, profit preference \(\gamma\), prices and costs, stock productivity and depletion used for conditioning.
Likely violations Catchability changing with technology or targeting; stocks whose dynamics are poorly described by Schaefer production; prices and costs that shift effort in ways not modelled.
Expected bias If \(r\) and \(K\) are taken from weak assessments, the MMSY and the risk of breaching Bpa inherit their bias; a shared-q simplification forces all rules along one line (\(F_i/F_j = q_i/q_j\)).
Warning signs Status quo that cannot be tuned to observed effort and depletions; MMSY that requires effort far outside historical levels; decision tables dominated by one stock at its safeguard.

Use and misuse

WarningMisuse warnings shown by the method selector
  • Caution: when goal = HCR testing: An assessment fit alone does not test a harvest control rule. Use the assessment as the estimation model in an MSE (see FLR mse).

Rules are maintained in data/misuse_rules.csv.

Field Entry
Pre-run checks To be completed
Concrete checks an app or analyst runs before fitting, with the action if a check fails (after Gutierrez and Kleisner, 2018).
Do not use when As a stock assessment: it does not estimate stock status from data. It needs status and productivity from assessments of the main stocks.
Common misuses Reading the safeguarded MMSY as a precise target rather than a comparison of options; ignoring the stocks held at their Bpa safeguard; skipping conditioning on current depletions.
Pre-use checklist Fleet tuned to the status quo; stocks conditioned on current depletion; sensitivity to the catchability matrix and \(\gamma\); rules tested with assessment and implementation error.
Advice suitability Harvest strategies for mixed fisheries (effort caps by segment, métier and seasonal closures, feedback rules); trade-off analysis for workshops.
